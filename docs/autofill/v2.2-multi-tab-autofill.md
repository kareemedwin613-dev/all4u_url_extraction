# Autofill in several tabs at once

Status: implemented (extension 1.9.0). Builds on [v2.0 guide-driven Autofill](v2.0-guide-driven-autofill.md).

## What changed for Appliers

- **Several jobs at once.** Start Autofill on one job tab, switch to another job tab, and start that one too. Each tab runs its own job.
- **Each tab shows its own status**, so nobody has to keep the side panel in view to know whether a page is done:

  | Where | Working | Done | Needs you | Stopped |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Toolbar badge (per tab) | `…` | `✓` | `!` | `×` |
  | Tab title prefix | ⏳ | ✓ | ⚠ | ✕ |
  | Notice on the page (top right, closes itself after 15 s) | — | "Autofill finished" | "Autofill needs your attention" | "Autofill stopped" |

  The notice gives the summary, for example *12 filled · 3 for you to answer · 1 waiting for this tab*.
- **An "Autofilling…" card in the middle of the tab while it works.** The page is lightly dimmed and the card shows the current step:
  1. Loading application
  2. Attaching resume
  3. Reading the form
  4. Filling fields

  Retries show only "Filling fields". When the job ends, the card is replaced by the corner notice. The card:
  - never takes focus;
  - lets every click, key press and scroll through to the page (search dropdowns only fill while the page keeps focus);
  - removes itself if no update arrives for 2 minutes, for example if the side panel was closed.

  Switching to a tab whose job is still running shows its card.
- **The side panel follows the tab you are looking at.** It shows that tab's job (banner and field list) at the top. Jobs in other tabs are listed under **Other tabs**, with **Open tab** and **Dismiss** buttons.
- **"Waiting for this tab".** Some search dropdowns (react-select, used by Greenhouse and Ashby, for example) only open in the tab that is in front. If a job runs in a background tab, those fields are left waiting and filled once, automatically, when you switch to the tab.

Keep the side panel open while jobs run: the panel drives each job. Closing a job tab ends its job.

## Autofill button on the job page

Job pages show a small **Autofill** button in the bottom-right corner. Clicking it opens a picker:

- **Likely this page**: the Applications whose job URL matches the tab. If the same job is assigned to several profiles, all of them are listed; the candidate name tells them apart.
- **Showing in the panel**: the rest of what My Applications is showing, with the panel's current filters.
- **Application number**: type a number and press Enter or **Go**. A number that isn't in the panel's list is looked up among your assigned Applications.

Picking one starts Autofill in that tab, with the same checks as the card's Autofill button. The tab's status then appears as above. The button's **×** hides it until the page is reloaded.

Where the button appears:

- on the main job sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, JazzHR, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters and others);
- on any site where an Application in the panel's list lives.

It appears only after My Applications has loaded in the panel, and it disappears after you sign out. The side panel must be open: if it isn't, the button opens it and asks you to pick again.

Safety:

- The job site's own code can't read or press the button.
- It reacts only to real clicks and key presses.
- It offers only Applications assigned to you.
- It only starts Autofill. It never touches the site's buttons and never submits.
- Job URLs are not passed to the page; only number, company, job title and candidate name are.

## Faster start

- The requests that do not depend on each other go out together: the field values, earlier recovery state, the cover letter and the Resume file.
- Status, recovery and telemetry writes no longer hold up the fill. They are queued per job and sent in the same order as before, in the background. A failed write never blocks the page.
- The Application details fetched when the Applier clicks **Autofill** are reused for two minutes instead of being requested again.

## How it works

| Piece | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Service worker | `chrome.storage.session.applicationSessions` holds one session per tab, replacing the single active session. Each session stores its own form frame. Starting a job on a tab replaces only that tab's earlier session. |
| Messages | `LIST_APPLICATION_SESSIONS` lists running sessions. `SET_APPLICATION_TAB_STATUS` sets the badge, title and notice; only the extension's own pages may send it. `RESET_ACTIVE_APPLICATION_SESSION` takes an optional `sessionId`. |
| Side panel | A map of jobs, keyed by session. Each job starts once, when its session appears, and runs independently of the others. |

Unchanged: the extension never clicks a submit button and never submits an application. A person reviews every page before submitting.
